# Hi, I'm Jinxin 👋

**Experimental Particle Physicist & Scientific Software Engineer**  
*Particle Physics · Scientific Computing · C++ / Python · AI Systems*

I'm a physicist and software engineer working at the intersection of **experimental particle physics, computational methods, and AI**. I earned my **Dr. rer. nat. in Physics** at Ruhr University Bochum, where my research focused on precision luminosity determination for the **PANDA experiment**.

I enjoy turning difficult scientific questions into quantitative models, reconstruction algorithms, and software that can be tested and understood.

## 🔬 Physics & Research

My doctoral research brought together detector physics, numerical methods, and large-scale simulation:

- **Precision luminosity measurements:** Studied antiproton–proton elastic scattering and the influence of extended target and residual-gas backgrounds on luminosity determination.
- **Particle tracking & vertex reconstruction:** Developed and evaluated methods for reconstructing displaced interaction vertices and correcting position-dependent reconstruction efficiencies.
- **Detector simulation:** Investigated HV-MAPS pixel-detector configurations through Monte Carlo simulation and quantitative reconstruction-performance comparisons.

These studies were carried out within the international **PANDA Collaboration**, using and extending its scientific software ecosystem.

## 💻 Software & AI Projects

- **[PANDA Research QA Agent](https://github.com/JinxinLee/PANDA_Agent)** — A research prototype for understanding scientific code, papers, and documentation through version-aware retrieval, evidence-grounded answers, and systematic evaluation.
- **[LuminosityFit](https://github.com/JinxinLee/LuminosityFit)** — A C++ framework for luminosity analysis in PANDA; I worked on its maintenance, extensions, and integration with simulation and fitting workflows.
- **[RestgasDetermination](https://github.com/JinxinLee/RestgasDetermination)** — PandaRoot-based simulation and reconstruction workflows for displaced particle tracks, interaction-vertex estimation, and residual-gas profile corrections.

**Tools and technologies:** C++, Python, ROOT / PandaRoot, Linux / HPC, Git, Docker, scientific simulation and data analysis; FastAPI, LangGraph, PostgreSQL, Qdrant, and retrieval-augmented generation.
