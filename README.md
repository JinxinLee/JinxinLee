### Selected research results

- **Luminosity determination:** An integrated reconstruction-and-fitting study achieved a **(0.05 ± 0.07)% relative deviation** from simulated ground truth under the studied conditions.
- **Iterative profile correction:** In a simulation stress test, global target-profile discrepancy decreased from **12.27% (iteration 1) to 1.30% (iteration 3)**.
